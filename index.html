THE PRODUCER

Anto Joseph

Anto Joseph is an Indian film producer and distributor known for his work in Malayalam cinema. He began his career in film production, made his producing debut with Big B in 2007, and founded Anto Joseph Film Company. His credits include Take Off, The Priest, and Malik.
